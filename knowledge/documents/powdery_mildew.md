# Powdery Mildew — Knowledge Summary

**Type:** Fungal disease (various erysiphalean fungi)
**Typical hosts in this knowledge base:** Rose, Apple

## Recognition
- White to gray powdery patches on upper leaf surfaces.
- Leaves may curl, yellow, or distort as infection progresses.

## Conditions that favor it
- High humidity combined with poor air circulation.
- Low light and crowded growth.

## Management actions (from the knowledge graph)
- Improve ventilation around the plant.
- Improve light exposure.
- Remove and dispose of heavily affected leaves.
