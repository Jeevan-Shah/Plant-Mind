# Late Blight — Knowledge Summary

**Type:** Oomycete disease (causal agent *Phytophthora infestans*)
**Typical hosts in this knowledge base:** Tomato, Potato

## Recognition
- Water-soaked, dark lesions on leaves, often with pale yellow margins.
- Rapid spread in cool, humid weather; leaves may wilt and collapse.

## Conditions that favor it
- High humidity and cool temperatures.
- Poor ventilation.
- Excess moisture on foliage.

## Management actions (from the knowledge graph)
- Remove affected leaves and plant parts promptly.
- Improve ventilation.
- Monitor disease progression closely — late blight develops fast.
