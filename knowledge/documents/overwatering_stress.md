# Overwatering Stress — Knowledge Summary

**Type:** Physiological (abiotic) stress
**Typical hosts in this knowledge base:** Any plant

## Recognition
- Yellowing leaves, often including newer growth.
- Wilting despite moist soil; leaves may curl.

## Conditions that favor it
- Excess moisture / waterlogged growing medium.
- Low light, which slows water uptake.

## Management actions (from the knowledge graph)
- Reduce excess watering; let the topsoil dry between waterings.
- Improve ventilation and light to help the growing medium dry evenly.
