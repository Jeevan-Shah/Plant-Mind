# General Preventive Plant Care — Knowledge Summary

Baseline preventive practices referenced across the PlantMind knowledge graph:

1. **Observe regularly.** Inspect leaves (top and underside) at least weekly and track changes over time.
2. **Water sensibly.** Water at the base, when the topsoil begins to dry; avoid wetting foliage unnecessarily.
3. **Ensure adequate light.** Match the plant's light requirement; inadequate light weakens plants and prolongs leaf wetness.
4. **Maintain airflow.** Space plants and prune dense growth to improve ventilation and reduce fungal pressure.
5. **Feed appropriately.** Fertilize at suitable rates during active growth; avoid both deficiency and excess.
6. **Sanitize.** Remove fallen or diseased leaves and dispose of them away from healthy plants.

> Demonstration summary for PlantMind V1; not an exhaustive agronomic reference.
