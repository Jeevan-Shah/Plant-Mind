# Leaf Spot — Knowledge Summary

**Type:** General fungal leaf-spotting condition
**Typical hosts in this knowledge base:** Tomato, Rose, Apple

## Recognition
- Discrete brown or black spots on leaves, sometimes with yellow halos.
- Severe cases lead to yellowing and premature leaf drop.

## Conditions that favor it
- Wet foliage and excess moisture.
- High humidity.

## Management actions (from the knowledge graph)
- Remove affected leaves.
- Reduce excess watering; water at the base, not the foliage.
