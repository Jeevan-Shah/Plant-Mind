# Early Blight — Knowledge Summary

**Type:** Fungal disease (causal agent commonly *Alternaria solani*)
**Typical hosts in this knowledge base:** Tomato, Potato

## Recognition
- Dark brown spots, often with concentric "target" rings.
- Surrounding tissue commonly turns yellow.
- Symptoms usually begin on older, lower leaves and move upward.

## Conditions that favor it
- High humidity around foliage.
- Poor ventilation / stagnant air.
- Extended leaf wetness.

## Management actions (from the knowledge graph)
- Remove affected leaves and dispose of them away from the growing area.
- Improve ventilation (spacing, pruning, air movement).
- Monitor disease progression: photograph and track symptoms over time.

## Prevention
- Maintain good airflow and appropriate plant spacing.
- Avoid working with wet foliage.
- Keep plants well nourished so they can resist infection.
