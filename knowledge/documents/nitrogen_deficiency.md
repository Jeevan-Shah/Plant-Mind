# Nitrogen Deficiency — Knowledge Summary

**Type:** Nutrient disorder
**Typical hosts in this knowledge base:** Any plant

## Recognition
- Uniform yellowing that typically starts on older, lower leaves.
- Reduced growth and pale overall coloration.

## Conditions that favor it
- Nutrient-poor growing medium or exhausted fertilizer.

## Management actions (from the knowledge graph)
- Nutrient management: apply a balanced fertilizer at the correct rate.
- Improve light so the plant can use available nutrients efficiently.
